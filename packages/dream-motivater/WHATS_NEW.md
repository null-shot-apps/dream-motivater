### ✨ Unlimited Custom Entries
You can now add **ANY** career goal or skill - not limited to predefined lists!

**Before:**
- Limited to ~20 predefined goals
- Limited to ~30 predefined skills
- No way to add custom entries

**After:**
- 100+ career goals in database
- 200+ skills in database
- **Unlimited custom entries** - add anything you want!

### 🔍 How It Works

When you search for a goal or skill:

1. **Type what you want** (e.g., "Freelance Photographer")
2. **See suggestions** from our extensive database
3. **Don't see it?** You'll see: `✨ Add "Your Entry" (custom)`
4. **Click to add** - Your custom entry is saved!
